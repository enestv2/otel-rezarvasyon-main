## Risks & open questions

- **Overview gets narrower:** use 60/40 width split and full-height panels; this retains city context and gives the near view substantially more area than the current 30% inset.
- **Long map titles or hotel names:** keep map panel titles short; allocate the flexible table width to the hotel name and let rows wrap instead of clipping.
- **Page overflow after larger map:** recommendation is to allow the ranked table to continue naturally to the next page, repeat its grouped header, and keep page numbers; do not shrink body typography to force one page.
